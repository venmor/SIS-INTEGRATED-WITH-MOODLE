## User behavior and UI/API/data flow

1. The appointed fictional coordinator prepares a complete period-wide master through the draft API. Server validation reports source, roster, coverage, seat, access, teaching-window, room, teacher, student and travel findings. Course previews derive from the same immutable master.
2. The staff publication page selects an academic period and traverses its saved proposals. Sessions and replacement changes use bounded pages. The review shows the saved manifest, permitted lecturer/delivery/access details, pinned policy and source references, current findings, the predecessor and changed/cancelled/allocation impact.
3. A different person holding the exact publication appointment supplies a reason, two unticked declarations and fresh password reconfirmation. The command binds its UUID, master digest, predecessor and decision payload. Source changes or a stale predecessor require a corrected version and renewed review.
4. Publication, current-version replacement, registration/attempt-linked projections, minimal audit and the durable owner event commit together. The previous publication remains current if the transaction fails. History is append-only; correction appends a new version.
5. The student's selected own attempt and period intersect the latest publication with current enrolled registrations. Drops, withdrawal, inactive entitlement and another attempt cannot grant stale class access. A newly registered course absent from the published manifest remains being prepared; registration remains valid.
6. The student page offers CAT week/day/list views, course filtering, bounded date and ten-row list traversal, publication/version/freshness, and plain-language changes. Unavailable or incomplete information does not become a false published timetable.

A successful default student read redirects to its explicit, server-confirmed attempt and period. Refresh recovery stores only an ISO last-confirmed date under an opaque account/attempt/period key. A failed read removes classes and history; identity uncertainty, a foreign/malformed response or an access refusal cannot reveal another context's date. This marker never supplies entitlement.

The current staff route is `/admin/timetabling/publications`; the student route is `/student/timetable`. The supported publication API is `GET /timetabling/demo-master/:id/publication-review` and `POST /timetabling/demo-master/:id/publish`. Period-scoped proposal history and publication history have separate bounded traversal. Browser proxies preserve session authentication, validate allowed paths/query context and require same-origin proof before adding the API's trusted mutation marker.

## Authority, recovery and audit

All publication and fictional student projection paths require demo opt-in and a loopback review/test/CI database boundary. Production stays denied. A central `TIMETABLE_COORDINATOR` at exact `SYSTEM:DEMO-UNIVERSITY` with `timetable-demo-master-draft` prepares/reads; an independent `DOMAIN_ADMIN` at that scope with `timetable-demo-master-publish` reviews/publishes. Setup, system-administration or teaching roles do not imply publication authority.

The service validates current appointment, scope, capability, account/person state and effective dates. It verifies the original proposer's person and appointment and rejects self-approval through another account. Relevant authority, credential and source rows are locked; late checks cover natural expiry. A new publication requires exactly one usable active password credential. Password values and hashes are excluded from stored commands, receipts, audit and events. The recorded assurance is `DEMO_PASSWORD_RECONFIRMATION`, not institutional MFA or a production digital signature.

Setup, rule and master writers coordinate with publication in setup-domain then period-lock order. Publication uses statement snapshots after those locks, so a waiter reads the preceding owner's committed source version. Delivery, period and registration parent rows are also locked against FK-linked section/calendar/roster appends. After the audit/outbox writes, the service repeats the full source/latest-rule check and then the actor/proposer authority checks before success. A retained source fingerprint does not replace those live checks.

An uncertain response preserves the exact nonsecret command and UUID, freezes target/reason, and queries that actor's matching receipt. Changed target, digest, predecessor or decision payload cannot reuse the reference. Current authority remains required for recovery; a recovered historical receipt is explicitly distinct from the current timetable. Failed source refresh disables a new approval and clears the declarations/password while preserving the last checked context.

The browser recovery record uses actor-and-appointment-scoped **sessionStorage**, containing only the bounded command/reason and proposal context. It excludes password, session token and roster. It supports reload and restored-tab recovery within the browser's tab session; it is not durable cross-device or new-session storage. Storage failure denies a new publication before POST. A future institutional workflow needs a governed server-side pending-command and retention contract.

The immutable master records proposer provenance, the manifest, source fingerprint and non-personal source context, including rule, calendar and institution-unit version references. Publication pins that master and stores its decision fingerprint, policy, assurance, reason, predecessor and impact. Projections retain course-registration, programme-attempt, period, section/offering/course-version, venue and teaching-assignment lineage. The additive source-context column leaves older proposals readable but unpublishable until a new source-validated version is prepared; it invents no historical provenance.

`DemoTimetablePublished` records a **pending Timetabling owner handoff** with `externalDelivery: NONE`. Its outbox row proves atomic handoff creation. It does not prove notification-owner processing, an in-system notice, delivery to a person or external calendar/Moodle synchronization.
