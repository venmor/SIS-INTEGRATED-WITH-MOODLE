# Product roadmap

The detailed executable backlog is [feature-sis-expansion-2.0](../../plan/feature-sis-expansion-2.0.md). It extends the approved release families with source-linked vertical slices, file targets, dependencies, user journeys and measurable exit gates.

First accept the integrated presentation MVP. Then deliver v1.1 academic depth; v1.2 teaching, timetable, examinations and Moodle; v1.3 finance; v1.4 protected support and a governed student-AI pilot; v1.5 quality/governance; v1.6 research completion, graduation and credentials; v1.7 reporting/regulatory; v1.8 production integrations; v2.0 operational acceptance.

Postgraduate delivery is deliberately distributed across candidature (v1.1), proposal/ethics (v1.2) and examination/completion (v1.6). SET participation proof is an early QA dependency of examination slips. Student AI and optional timetable optimization require their own approved ADRs and cannot bypass official decisions.

See [source authority](SOURCE-MAP.md), [current maturity](MODULE-MATURITY.md), [release gates](RELEASE-PLAN.md), [deferrals](DEFERRED-FEATURES.md) and [technical debt](TECHNICAL-DEBT.md). None of these documents approve production policy or a deployment.
