## Execution decisions

- Reuse source owners and existing commands. A browser batch orchestrates independently idempotent case claims; it does not introduce a long-running HTTP transaction or a new admission decision endpoint. Every case has its own outcome; uncertainty stops subsequent calls.
- Course-version and offering models already exist, but that is not evidence of a completed configuration workspace. Keep that dependency visible before advanced academic consumers are declared complete.
- Synthetic policy choices are versioned and editable through their owner workflows. Technical limits are not presented as UNZA regulations. A working sample configuration closes a demonstration slice only when normal, denial, conflict and recovery paths are tested.
- Do not count task headings, routes, tables or historical green runs as completed product features. Update this sequence and the maturity matrix with evidence after each slice.
