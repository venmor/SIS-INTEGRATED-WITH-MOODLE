# Method and Design Process

The design was developed iteratively through five connected views:

1. **Institutional view:** stakeholders, authority, policy, segregation of duties and record ownership.
2. **Domain view:** entities, lifecycles, invariants, commands, events and external boundaries.
3. **Experience view:** role workspaces, end-to-end journeys, handoffs, language, errors and accessibility.
4. **Engineering view:** modular architecture, configuration, security, resilience, Git/CI/CD and versioning.
5. **Evidence view:** requirements, traceability, tests, approvals, learning records and presentation proof.

Each major design part was presented, revised where necessary and approved in the shared conversation. The recovered standalone outputs are preserved as active sources in `15-APPROVED-DESIGN-EVIDENCE/`. This final handbook reorganizes them by reader goal rather than conversation order while retaining source lineage.

Implementation will continue the same process through small vertical slices. Every slice begins with a task packet, links to controlling design, assigns a developer and reviewer, records tests/evidence and ends with a learning note and demonstration.
