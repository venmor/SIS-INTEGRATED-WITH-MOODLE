# Proposed System and User Experience

The proposed platform provides public discovery, applicant/student self-service, role-specific staff workspaces and controlled operational queues. A person may hold several roles, but must deliberately switch workspace; permissions are evaluated against identity, active role, scope, assignment, record state, purpose and time-bound authority.

The experience is structured around tasks and records rather than departments. Every critical status communicates what happened, what it means, who owns the next step and what the user can do. Forms save drafts where policy allows, preserve valid input after failure, review high-impact submissions before commitment and prevent duplicate actions.

Staff users receive decisions and obligations first. Tables support professional comparison; cards represent tasks or records; charts appear only when they support a real decision and expose population, period, freshness, source and limitations. Sensitive support, discipline and finance information is separated from ordinary academic views.
