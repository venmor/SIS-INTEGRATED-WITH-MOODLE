# Role Blueprint 6 — School Dean and Dean of Students

> **Source mode:** composite approved blueprint. This book reorganizes the approved Dean experience in Section 12A/12B, role/scope rules, restricted-information matrix and governance tests. It adds no institutional authority beyond those sources.

## Role separation

The School/Faculty Dean governs academic decisions and performance within an organizational scope. The Dean of Students governs student-affairs services, welfare capacity, accommodation, orientation, student governance and formally escalated support matters. These are different workspaces and neither is an unrestricted super-role.

If one person holds several appointments, the header always states the active context, for example: **Dean workspace · School of Engineering · 2026 Academic Year**. Switching from Lecturer to Dean is deliberate, effective-dated and audited.

## School Dean home

The page is ordered by:

1. Decisions requiring personal authority
2. School academic obligations at risk
3. Student-success work awaiting school action
4. Department/programme exceptions
5. Delegations and follow-up
6. Academic and governance deadlines

A decision package identifies the matter, affected population, referring office, policy/version, evidence, recommendations, conflict checks, deadline, prior decisions and permitted outcomes. The Dean may approve, decline, return for clarification, refer or delegate only where configured authority permits. The Dean cannot rewrite evidence while deciding.

## School oversight journey

1. Open a decision or obligation from the Dean queue.
2. Confirm active role, school scope, authority period and conflict-of-interest state.
3. Review evidence freshness, recommendations and consequences.
4. Request clarification when evidence is incomplete; the item remains pending with an owner/deadline.
5. Preview the formal decision and affected downstream processes.
6. Confirm with reason and policy version.
7. Record an immutable decision event and notify responsible offices.
8. Track conditions, delegated tasks or escalation to the correct higher authority.

## Dean of Students home

The page emphasizes service obligations rather than private case content:

- Welfare and counselling referral demand
- First-contact waiting times and overdue work
- Accommodation/residence issues
- Orientation and student engagement
- Student-governance actions
- Disciplinary process obligations
- Formally escalated safeguarding/welfare matters
- Service capacity, deadlines and policy actions

Aggregate status is the default. Individual identity appears only for an assigned or formally escalated purpose. Counselling session notes, diagnoses and personal disclosures are not exposed through management metrics.

## Delegation

Delegation names the recipient, scope, permitted action, whether the delegate recommends or decides, start/end time, reason and revocation route. It cannot expand the delegator’s own authority and expires automatically. Delegated decisions retain both acting and delegating identities in audit.

## Information boundaries

| Role | Permitted | Prohibited |
|---|---|---|
| School Dean | School trends, academic escalations, authorized decision packages | Browsing counselling notes; editing official results or finance records |
| Dean of Students | Service demand, formally escalated welfare/discipline records, capacity actions | Routine access to counselling-session content or unrelated academic cases |
| Deputy/Assistant Dean | Explicit delegated scope and time only | Inheriting all Dean access by title alone |
| Department/Programme leaders | Their scoped academic actions and aggregates | Institution-wide support or private-case browsing |

## Failure and recovery

- Stale evidence blocks a final adverse decision and provides a refresh/clarification route.
- Authority expiry during review preserves notes but blocks submission.
- Concurrent decisions produce a version conflict and show the committed outcome.
- Failed notifications do not reverse a valid decision; delivery is retried and visible.
- A delegated task that expires returns to the owning queue.
- A metric without a definition, population, period, source and freshness cannot support a formal decision.

## Acceptance requirements

- Each Dean receives a unique task-oriented workspace.
- The system distinguishes appointment, active role, scope and delegation.
- Aggregates do not become unrestricted personal-record access.
- Counselling confidentiality survives Dean-level oversight.
- Every formal decision, evidence access, delegation and export is audited.
- Negative tests deny access outside organizational scope and authority period.
- Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, loading, stale, empty, denied and conflict states are designed.
