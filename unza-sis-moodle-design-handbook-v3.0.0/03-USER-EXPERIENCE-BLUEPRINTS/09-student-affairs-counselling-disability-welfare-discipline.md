# Role Blueprint 9 — Student Affairs and Restricted Support Services

> **Source mode:** composite approved blueprint using Design Section 8, Section 12B, adviser/student support journeys and restricted-record permissions.

## Distinct roles

Counsellor, Disability-support Officer, Student-welfare Officer, Accommodation Officer, Disciplinary Case Officer/Committee Member, Counselling Service Lead and Dean of Students have separate workspaces. Sharing a case framework does not create shared unrestricted visibility.

## Student-facing principles

- Support is voluntary and respectful except where a separately approved safety/discipline rule applies.
- Academic observations are explainable events, not diagnoses or unexplained risk scores.
- Notifications sent outside the portal are neutral and reveal minimal sensitive detail.
- Students can see the purpose, recipient, next step, agreed actions and how to correct inaccurate information.
- Declining ordinary support does not create a disciplinary record.

## Journey A — support observation and referral

1. A permitted academic/support signal creates an explainable observation with source and freshness.
2. An authorized human triages it as monitor, academic follow-up, offer another service, merge or dismiss.
3. The adviser contacts the student through an approved template and separates internal from student-visible text.
4. The student may book, request contact, choose another service, decline or report resolution.
5. Counselling referral is created only after acceptance/self-referral or approved safeguarding route.
6. The receiving service gets minimum necessary context and records its own restricted case.
7. The referring role receives only permitted service status, not session content.

## Counsellor workspace

The Counsellor sees assigned/self-referral cases, triage tasks, appointments, consent, required follow-up and safety obligations. Restricted notes have their own access log, retention/classification and disclosure route. An unassigned counsellor receives a denial that does not expose case details.

## Disability and welfare workspaces

These roles manage requests, evidence, consent, service decisions, accommodations/support plans and implementation status within purpose. Academic users see only the approved accommodation or action necessary to support the student, not diagnosis or unrelated evidence.

## Discipline workspace

Discipline records are separated from counselling and ordinary academic support. The workflow preserves allegation, notice, evidence, response, hearing/committee authority, decision, appeal and disclosure rules. An academic difficulty cannot be silently converted into misconduct.

## Accommodation workspace

Accommodation handles application/assignment/waitlist, residence issue and authorized welfare escalation. It receives only the minimum information required for housing decisions. Capacity and waiting-time reporting is aggregate by default.

## Restricted-information controls

- Relationship, assignment, purpose and case state are checked on every access.
- Bulk export is disabled or separately authorized for sensitive cases.
- Break-glass access is time-limited, reasoned, monitored and reviewed.
- Search does not reveal unassigned case existence.
- Reports are aggregated/pseudonymized unless identifiable disclosure is approved.
- Audit logs protect integrity but do not copy full sensitive notes into general logs.

## Failure and recovery

- No verified contact method creates a contact-update/manual route, not a false delivered state.
- Duplicate outreach links to the existing case.
- Full service capacity offers alternatives and urgent-support information defined by policy.
- Access/assignment changes during composition block disclosure and preserve an audit-safe draft.
- Appointment provider failure preserves the request and offers alternative scheduling.
- Stale observation data is visible and cannot support unsupported escalation.

## Acceptance requirements

- Academic, counselling, disability, welfare, accommodation and discipline records remain separated.
- Every individual disclosure has role, relationship, purpose and scope.
- Dean/service leads can manage capacity without routine note access.
- Students receive plain, non-accusatory explanations and response choices.
- Negative authorization tests cover unrelated students, unassigned cases and excessive exports.
- Accessibility and privacy tests cover notifications, scheduling, forms and case status.
