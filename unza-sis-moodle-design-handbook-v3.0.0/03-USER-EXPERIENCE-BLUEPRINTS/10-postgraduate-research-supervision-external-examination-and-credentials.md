# Role Blueprint 10 — Postgraduate Research, Supervision, External Examination and Credentials

> **Source mode:** composite approved blueprint using the complete postgraduate research domain, student journeys, academic role rules, assessment governance and graduation/credential controls.

## Roles

| Role | Purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Postgraduate Researcher | Complete proposal, ethics, milestones, thesis/examination and corrections | Cannot approve own milestones or examiner outcome |
| Research Supervisor | Guide assigned researchers and record recommendations/progress | Cannot act outside assignment or replace committee/examiner authority |
| Research Coordinator/Graduate School | Manage candidature obligations, assignments, committees and deadlines | Cannot manufacture evidence or examiner decisions |
| Ethics/Research Committee Member | Review evidence and decide within configured committee authority | Conflict-of-interest cases are blocked/reassigned |
| External Examiner | Securely review assigned submission and submit independent report | Minimum, time-limited access only |
| Graduation/Credentials Officer | Govern completion, award/conferral and credentials | Reads approved sources; cannot alter source results |

## Researcher home

The page presents candidature status, current milestone, supervisor team, ethics state, next required action, deadline, submitted evidence, returned items, fees/holds relevant to the stage and a history timeline. It must never show only “research in progress.”

## Journey A — proposal and ethics

1. Researcher creates a versioned proposal draft and submits required evidence.
2. Supervisor reviews and records recommendation/comments without overwriting the submission.
3. Committee/authorized role checks scope, conflicts, evidence and policy version.
4. Decision is approved, returned with specific changes or declined with permitted explanation.
5. Material proposal change triggers impact review and, where required, ethics amendment.
6. Research activity that requires clearance cannot be marked active before approval.

## Journey B — supervision and milestones

Supervisor assignments are effective-dated, capacity-aware and auditable. Each milestone identifies criteria, evidence, due date, responsible reviewer and outcomes. Researcher response, supervisor recommendation and final approval are distinct. Extensions/interruption/return require reason, evidence and authority.

## Journey C — thesis submission and examination

Readiness checks include required milestones, approved title/scope, ethics, similarity/format checks where policy requires, fees/clearance, supervisor recommendation and outstanding holds. A non-recommendation follows an explicit route and does not silently prevent a permitted submission.

Examiner nomination, conflict declaration, secure appointment, controlled document access, report submission, viva/committee decision and corrections are separate states. External access expires and all downloads/submissions are audited.

## Journey D — corrections, completion and credentials

Corrections use categories, required evidence, deadline and reviewer. Resubmission creates a new version. Final completion produces an approved outcome that feeds award-readiness; it does not directly create a certificate. Graduation/credentials then verifies programme requirements, official results, approvals and clearance before award/conferral and credential generation.

## Failure and recovery

- Lost connection during thesis/report submission uses idempotency and a receipt/status check.
- Uploaded documents are quarantined and scanned before reviewer access.
- Examiner conflict or access expiry blocks viewing and routes reassignment.
- Supervisor change preserves history and creates a formal handover.
- Deadline/configuration changes are effective-dated and do not rewrite prior obligations.
- Failed credential delivery does not reverse conferral; replacement/revocation have separate records.

## Acceptance requirements

- Researcher, supervisor, committee and examiner actions remain independent and traceable.
- Confidential examination material is scoped, time-limited and audited.
- Approved states cannot be silently overwritten.
- Research milestones and delays show owner, reason, deadline and next action.
- Graduation/credentials consume approved records and cannot correct them directly.
- Denial, conflict, duplicate submission, unsafe file, stale policy, recovery and accessibility tests pass.
