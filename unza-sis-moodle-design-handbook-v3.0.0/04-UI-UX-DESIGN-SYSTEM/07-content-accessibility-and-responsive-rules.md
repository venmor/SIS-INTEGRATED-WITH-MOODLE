# Content, Accessibility and Inclusive Use

## Content standard

Write for the user’s task, not the system’s internal implementation. Use familiar university terms defined in the glossary and keep technical identifiers secondary.

Message pattern:

```text
What happened
What it means
What happens next or what the user must do
Reference/help route
```

Dates and deadlines include the institutional time zone. Money includes currency. Status never relies only on colour or an icon.

## Required accessibility states

For every critical journey test:

- Keyboard-only completion
- Screen-reader announcement and labelling
- Logical heading and focus order
- Visible focus indication
- Zoom/reflow without lost actions or horizontal workflow scrolling
- Accessible form error identification and correction
- Mobile-browser completion
- Slow/intermittent connection recovery
- Accessible sign-in and account recovery
- Alternative to charts and inaccessible document content

## Inclusive privacy

Accessible alternatives must preserve the same confidentiality boundary. For example, screen-reader labels and accessible document text must not expose fields hidden visually from the active role.

## Content ownership

Notices, declarations, email/SMS templates and official labels have template IDs, versions, owners, effective dates and approval history. Developers must not embed unapproved policy wording directly into components.
