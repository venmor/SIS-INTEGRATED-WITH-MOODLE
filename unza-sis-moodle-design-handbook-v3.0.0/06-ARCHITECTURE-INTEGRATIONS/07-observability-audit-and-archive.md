# Observability, Audit and Archive

## Operational observability

Logs, metrics and traces identify request/correlation ID, module, operation, outcome, duration and safe error category. They exclude passwords, tokens, full payment credentials, sensitive counselling notes and unnecessary personal data.

Dashboards monitor service health, request/error rates, queue age, failed deliveries, reconciliation backlog, database health, storage, backup status and critical journey indicators. Alerts must name an owner and response runbook; noisy alerts are reviewed.

## Audit

Material actions record actor, active role, scope, purpose, target, prior/new version references, policy/configuration version, decision/reason, time, correlation and result. Audit is immutable and searchable only by authorized roles. Audit records prove what happened; they must not become a second unrestricted copy of sensitive content.

## Archive and retention

Retention schedules are configuration approved by the institution. Archive preserves authenticity, metadata, access controls, legal holds and retrieval evidence. Disposal requires authority, reason, affected record class and audit; ordinary application deletion is not used for official academic/financial history.
