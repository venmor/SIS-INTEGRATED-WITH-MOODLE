# Step 11 — Expansion from MVP to Complete System

Expansion remains vertical and evidence-gated. Do not create empty modules merely to claim coverage.

| Release family | Expansion outcome | Representative roles/journeys | Required operational evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v1.1` Student/academic depth | Add/drop, exemptions, repeats, progression, appeals, transcript basis | Student, adviser, registry, examinations | Policy boundary/regression and amendment tests |
| `v1.2` Teaching/Moodle depth | Teaching assignment, TGs, calendars, real test Moodle and grade mapping | Lecturer/tutor, coordinator, Moodle admin | Contract, drift, archive and load evidence |
| `v1.3` Finance depth | Sponsorship, partial payment, arrangements, refunds, reversals, reports | Student, finance, sponsor | Money, approval, provider and restore tests |
| `v1.4` Student support | Counselling, disability, welfare, accommodation, safeguarding boundaries | Student, adviser, support, Dean of Students | Restricted-data, consent, denial and service UAT |
| `v1.5` QA/governance | Reviews, evidence, findings, actions, committees, leadership packages | QAO, owners, leadership, auditor | Independence, evidence/version and closure tests |
| `v1.6` Graduation/credentials | Readiness, award/conferral, certificate/transcript verification | Student, registry, Senate/graduation | Source-integrity, replacement/revocation tests |
| `v1.7` Reporting/regulatory | Metrics, certified packages, signatory delivery, correction | Reporting user, leadership, regulator/auditor | Privacy suppression, signatory, acknowledgement tests |
| `v1.8` Production integrations | Approved identity, Moodle, payment, email/SMS, storage providers | IAM, Moodle/integration operations | Credentials, monitoring, reconciliation and incident evidence |
| `v2.0` Operational completion | All agreed modules meet declared operational maturity | All approved roles | Security, accessibility, load, restore, observability and UAT evidence |

## Rules for every expansion

1. Confirm a user problem and approved role journey.
2. Update requirement/action/permission/test traceability.
3. Add effective-dated configuration rather than hard-coded policy.
4. Extend contracts additively where possible and plan deprecation.
5. Preserve regression coverage for all MVP journeys.
6. Test migration/rollback and cross-module consequences.
7. Record new learning and presentation evidence.
8. Update module maturity honestly: designed, scaffolded, functional or operational.
