# Master Contents and Required Reading Order

| Step | Folder | Purpose | Primary reader |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | `00-ORIENTATION` | Scope, authority, terminology and reading routes | Everyone |
| 1 | `01-FINAL-PROJECT-REPORT` | Report-ready narrative from problem to conclusion | Developers, supervisor, assessors |
| 2 | `02-INSTITUTIONAL-AND-SYSTEM-DESIGN` | Approved institutional/domain foundation | Architects, AI agents |
| 3 | `03-USER-EXPERIENCE-BLUEPRINTS` | Role-by-role journeys, actions, errors and handoffs | Developers, UX, testers |
| 4 | `04-UI-UX-DESIGN-SYSTEM` | Visual constitution, screens, components and accessibility | Frontend lead, reviewers |
| 5 | `05-REQUIREMENTS-PERMISSIONS-DATA` | Requirements, permissions, actions, policies and data rules | Backend, database, security |
| 6 | `06-ARCHITECTURE-INTEGRATIONS` | Modules, state, contracts, Moodle/provider boundaries | Architecture, backend, operations |
| 7 | `07-SECURITY-PRIVACY-RESILIENCE` | Security, rate limiting, redundancy, recovery and code health | Both developers, reviewers |
| 8 | `08-ENGINEERING-DELIVERY` | Locked stack, future repo, Git, CI/CD, release and OS rules | Both developers |
| 9 | `09-AI-AGENT-OPERATING-MANUAL` | Context, scope, task and evidence rules for AI | AI agents and human reviewers |
| 10 | `10-DEVELOPER-LEARNING-AND-TEAMWORK` | Charles/Chitindu learning and rotation model | Both developers |
| 11 | `11-STEP-BY-STEP-IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP` | Design gate, MVP releases and expansion to completion | Team and supervisor |
| 12 | `12-TESTING-AND-ACCEPTANCE` | Proof required for features and releases | Test lead, reviewer |
| 13 | `13-DEMO-AND-PRESENTATION` | Seeded demo, stories, evidence and defence preparation | Presenters |
| 14 | `14-TEMPLATES` | Reusable task, ADR, PR, learning, release and incident records | Developers and AI agents |
| 15 | `15-APPROVED-DESIGN-EVIDENCE` | Active exact design requirements and their approvals | Developers, AI agents, testers, reviewers |
| 16 | `90-TRACEABILITY-AND-GOVERNANCE` | Coverage, decisions, changes and validation | Team, supervisor, auditor |

The development route includes the task-linked exact approved records as well as curated handbooks. Read the relevant individual evidence files completely; do not load the whole compendium into every AI prompt. Check the authority order and supersession register before resolving a disagreement between a summary and its source.
